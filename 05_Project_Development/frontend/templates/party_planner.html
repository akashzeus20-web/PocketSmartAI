How Our Event Math Works

Organizing an event requires balancing multiple cost buckets so you never run out of food, seating, or ambiance:

  • Catering & Beverages (~45%): Quality appetizers, main courses, desserts, and drink stations.
  • Venue & Rentals (~20%): Seating, folding tables, linens, and space rental fees.
  • Decor & Ambiance (~20%): Cohesive themed backdrops, ambient lighting, and table runners.
  • Entertainment & Favors (~15%): Sound system, interactive games, and guest keepsakes.
✨ Zero Overrun: PocketSmart AI checks (Total Estimated Cost ≤ Total Budget) and generates a per-head metric ($/guest) for clear planning.
