### 2. Key Differentiators of PocketSmart AI
1. **Budget-First Generative AI**: Unlike standard conversational chatbots that generate text without verifying math, PocketSmart AI forces AI prompts to operate within explicit JSON schemas and validates that the sum of line items does not violate the ceiling constraint.
2. **Vision-Driven Jewelry Harmonization**: Users do not need to articulate complex color theory; uploading an outfit photo extracts dominant tones, complementary secondary accents, and silhouette geometry.
3. **Transparent Ethics**: In accordance with the system brief, the platform transparently indicates whether an item link is a verified live integration or an AI-modeled realistic market sample.
